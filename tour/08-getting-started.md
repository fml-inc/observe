The whole setup, in order:

1. `fml install` — wire up collection, sync, and the Claude Code plugin (you've likely done this — it's how the tour got here)
2. `fml login` — sign in (`fml login --device` inside a sandbox)
3. `fml org <slug>` — pick your organization, if you have more than one
4. `fml doctor` — verify collection, sync, and plugin registration are healthy
5. `fml open` — open the dashboard and connect your first integration
6. Back in Claude Code, try: "what did I work on this week?" or "check Sentry for new errors"

FML gets more useful as more of your team installs it — shared history, shared skills, org-wide spend visibility.

When something looks wrong: `fml doctor` first.
