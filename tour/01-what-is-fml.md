title: What is FML?
order: 1
---
FML is an intelligence layer for AI-assisted engineering.

Every agent session starts from zero: it re-reads your codebase, rediscovers your conventions, and burns tokens learning things your team already knows. FML fixes that.

It does three things:

1. **Observes** your agent sessions locally — what was asked, what ran, what it cost.
2. **Connects** your team's tools — Slack, Linear, Sentry, GitHub and more — so agents pull real context mid-task.
3. **Injects** knowledge — skills, memory, and analysis — so each session starts warm instead of cold.

The next two lessons cover where your data goes and the three ways to use FML day to day.
