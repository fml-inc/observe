Skills are your org's reusable knowledge, loaded on demand: how to run the deploy, what the review checklist is, how the billing system fits together.

- `fml_list_skills` — browse what your org has published
- `fml_load_skill` — pull one into the current conversation

Instead of every engineer re-explaining the same context to their agent, someone writes it once as a skill and every agent in the org loads it when relevant.
